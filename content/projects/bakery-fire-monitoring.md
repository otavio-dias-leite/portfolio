## Project overview

A technical project focused on monitoring fire-related risks in a bakery environment. The goal is to support early awareness of potentially dangerous conditions in a workplace where heat-generating equipment is part of daily operation.

## Technical focus

- Monitoring of fire-related risk conditions.
- Electronic monitoring and alert-system concepts.
- Application of technical solutions to a real work environment.
